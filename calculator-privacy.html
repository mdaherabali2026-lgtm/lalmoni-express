Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the SAS9 Advanced Electronic Calculator (Model 1654X) mobile application.

1. Overview & Offline Data Privacy

SAS9 Advanced Electronic Calculator (Model 1654X) is built with an offline-first architecture. All standard arithmetic operations, grand totals, memory storage (M+/M-/MRC), check-and-correct records, and Loan/EMI simulations are calculated and stored 100% locally on your device. We do not transmit, sell, rent, or collect your calculation numbers, personal financial records, or personal identifiable information (PII).

2. Google AdMob Advertising

This app displays banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to support free distribution on the Google Play Store.

Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), general device models, operating system version, and anonymous performance analytics in compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies. This data is used solely to serve relevant, non-intrusive advertisements and monitor ad performance.

For more information on how Google manages advertising data, please review Google's Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Device Permissions Used

This application requests only the minimum essential permissions:

  • INTERNET: Strictly required to retrieve and display banner advertisements from Google AdMob.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used by the advertising SDK to determine whether device connectivity is available.

No sensitive permissions (such as Camera, Microphone, GPS Location, Contacts, or External Storage) are requested or accessed by this application.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application is a general-purpose electronic utility suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe your child has inadvertently provided personal information, please contact us so we can take immediate action.

5. User Control & Data Deletion

You maintain complete control over your data. Your calculation history is stored solely in local app storage. You can instantly and permanently erase all saved calculation history at any time by tapping the 'CLEAR HISTORY' button in the History tab, or by clearing the app data in Android System Settings.
